Amply v1.7.2
Release date: 2026-09-27

Ratings
- New "Rate Amply" button in Settings > App Version. Store installs show Windows' own rating
  dialog without leaving Amply; other installs open the Microsoft Store review page.
- After a week of use and 30 plays, Store installs may show one small "Enjoying Amply?" card.
  "Not now" snoozes it for a month, "Don't ask again" ends it, and it never appears more than
  three times, during Game Mode, or after you have rated.
