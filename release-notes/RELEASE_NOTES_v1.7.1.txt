Amply v1.7.1
Release date: 2026-09-26

Artist details
- Artist bios and photos now come from the right artist. Amply matches the songs in your library
  to the artist on MusicBrainz and follows that artist's own link to Wikipedia, instead of
  searching Wikipedia by name. Artists who share a name with a city, a composer or another band
  (Berlin, Bellini, Player, Nirvana, Drake, Shaggy) now show the right page.
- Song, album and list pages are never shown as an artist bio.
- When no page can be tied to an artist, the panel stays empty instead of showing a stranger.
- Artist names in any script resolve (for example 千葉雄喜 -> Yuki Chiba).
- Profiles saved by earlier versions are re-checked automatically in the background.
- Artist photos served from Wikipedia's new thumbnail host load again.
- Genre and artwork lookups compare whole artist names, so "Drake" no longer matches "Drake Bell".

Library and playback
- Every listed format plays (.wav, .ogg, .aiff, ALAC .m4a); unreadable files and missing drives
  no longer stop a scan.
- 49 broken titles healed (e.g. "Hotel California" no longer shows as "2013"); titles in every
  script are kept intact.

Lyrics
- Fewer requests, stricter matching, and clear states for instrumental tracks and offline mode.

Window
- Fits small and portrait screens, runs as a single instance, and detects real fullscreen apps.
